🧪 How to test a transfer

  1. Create a second player using “New player”.
  2. Copy that player's SIMUPI ID.
  3. Switch back to your profile.
  4. Open Pay and send a test amount.
  5. Switch to the receiver profile and check the new balance and history.
These profiles share this browser's local storage. They are not online accounts and cannot receive money from another device.
